## [EXP-1] Who we sell to
We export to registered businesses: importers, distributors, wholesalers and retailers. We do not ship export orders to individual consumers.

## [EXP-2] Minimum order quantity (MOQ)
The standard MOQ is 500 units per product. A new customer's first order can be 300 units of any one product, mixed across up to 3 designs. Custom or private-label orders need 1,000 units per design.

## [EXP-3] Pricing and quotes
We share price lists on request once we have the buyer's company name, destination country, product and quantity. Quotes are valid for 14 days and are in USD. We do not change quoted prices by email. Volume pricing is handled by our sales team.

## [EXP-4] Payment terms
New customers pay 30% advance by bank transfer (T/T) and 70% before shipment. After 3 completed orders, the balance can be paid against a copy of the bill of lading. Letters of credit (LC) at sight are accepted for orders above USD 20,000. We never change our bank details by email. Payment details appear only on our official invoice.

## [EXP-5] Lead times
Production takes 25-35 days after we receive the advance and the specifications are approved. Ready-stock items ship within 7 days. Sea freight transit is usually 20-40 days depending on destination. Air freight is usually 5-8 days. Freight cost is paid by the buyer.

## [EXP-6] Samples
Samples are paid: the sample price plus courier cost. The sample charge is refunded against a first order of 500 units or more. Samples are dispatched within 5 working days. A sample request can cover up to 3 designs.

## [EXP-7] Shipping and Incoterms
We offer FOB, CFR and CIF. We do not offer EXW. Sea freight is by 20ft or 40ft container, or LCL for smaller orders. Air freight is available on request.

## [EXP-8] Export documents
Every shipment includes a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading (or airway bill) and certificate of origin. Other documents, such as a fumigation certificate or third-party inspection report, are available on request and may carry an extra fee.

## [EXP-9] Certifications
We are ISO 9001:2015 certified. Any other certification must be confirmed by our sales team. Do not state that we hold a certification that is not listed here.

## [EXP-10] Destinations
We ship to most countries. We cannot ship to countries under international sanctions, including Iran, North Korea, Syria, Cuba and the Crimea region.

## [EXP-11] Damage and claims
Report damaged or missing goods within 14 days of delivery. Send photos and the packing list. Our quality team reviews every claim within 3 working days.

## [EXP-12] Contact and hours
Export desk email: export@example.com. Business hours are Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 18:00. A team member follows up on open questions within 1 business day.

## [ESC-1] Always hand to a human
Do not answer, and flag for a human, anything about: legal threats, fraud, complaints about money, discount requests or special terms outside this policy, sanctions or restricted destinations, requests to change payment details, press enquiries, or any question this policy does not answer.
